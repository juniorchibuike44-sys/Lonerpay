Privacy Policy

How we handle your information when you use LonerPay.

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Who we are and how to contact us

LonerPay is operated by LONERHUB LIMITED, a Nigerian company (RC 9900283). This policy covers our website, customer account, wallet, bill-payment services and Business Tools.

For privacy questions, corrections or deletion requests, email support@lonerpay.com or lonerhublimited@gmail.com. You can also call +234 913 112 3780 or visit Support.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, phone number, profile details and authentication records.
  • Payments and services: wallet balances, funding records, amounts, recipient phone numbers, meter or TV account identifiers, selected bundles, provider references, status, fees and receipts.
  • Verification: identity details supplied when requesting a supported virtual account or verification service, including a BVN where required. These are processed with the relevant verification/payment provider. Do not send identity numbers through support messages.
  • Security: authentication tokens, approved-device records, PIN verification records, passkey public credentials, request IP addresses and technical logs used to protect accounts and investigate failures.
  • Business Tools: merchant and customer contact details, invoices, recorded repayments, credit records, stock information, store details and product photographs you upload.
  • Support and communications: messages, account-deletion requests and notification delivery records.

Why we use information

We use information to provide the services you request, authenticate access, process and reconcile payments, display receipts, maintain Business Tools, answer support requests, prevent abuse and meet applicable obligations. We use permission-based features only when you choose them. We may send service, security and account notices; optional communications depend on the preferences available in your account.

Payment cards, biometrics and voice

Card payment details are entered through Paystack’s checkout. LonerPay’s own forms do not ask for your card number or CVV. Payment references and relevant transaction details are retained for reconciliation.

Phone-security login uses your device’s supported passkey/biometric mechanism. LonerPay does not receive your fingerprint image or face scan; the device performs the biometric check.

Voice control listens only after you tap Speak and grant browser permission. Your browser may send audio to its speech-recognition service, subject to that provider’s privacy practices. The current voice-control feature does not upload or store recordings or transcripts on LonerPay’s servers. Recognised text is held temporarily in the command panel. Voice receipt playback uses the device/browser speech service. Voice commands do not authorise payments.

Who receives information

  • Payment and bill-payment providers: Paystack, Pairgate, VTU.ng and VTpass, as applicable to the service and provider currently enabled, receive the details necessary to fund wallets, verify customers or fulfil purchases.
  • Infrastructure: Supabase provides authentication, database and file storage; Vercel hosts the service; Zoho handles support email. These providers process relevant data to deliver their services and may process it outside Nigeria.
  • People you choose to share with: recipients of invoices, receipts and reminders. Published WhatsApp Store listings expose your store contact details, selected product information and photos to people with the link. WhatsApp and other sharing apps handle information under their own policies.
  • Authorised staff and authorities: access is limited to operational needs or lawful requests, fraud prevention and dispute handling.

We do not sell your personal information. Merchants must have an appropriate basis for entering their customers’ information and sending reminders; avoid uploading unnecessary or sensitive information.

Storage, cookies and security

We use HTTPS, authenticated server requests and access controls. Your browser/device stores session and approved-device information, preferences, cached bundle catalogues and certain receipt information. Some local records may remain until cleared from that device. Signing out is different from deleting an account. Use your browser’s site-data controls when removing local data from a shared device.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device and never share passwords, OTPs, login PINs or payment PINs. Report suspected misuse promptly.

Retention and deletion

Account and business records are kept while needed to provide your account and requested services. When you request deletion, we verify ownership, resolve any remaining wallet funds or pending matters with you, then remove account access and delete or anonymise associated information that is no longer required. Deletion is not simply account freezing.

Payment, accounting, verification, security and dispute records may be retained where needed for applicable legal obligations, fraud prevention or unresolved claims. Retained records are restricted to those purposes. The relevant period depends on the record and obligation; support will explain any exception and applicable retention period when processing your request. We ask relevant service providers to remove associated information unless they have their own lawful retention obligations. Backups and provider-held records may follow their own retention cycles.

We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days, or explain any outstanding issue and expected next step. This is a service target, not a promise that all legally required financial records will be erased in that period.

Your choices and requests

You may ask to access or correct information, request deletion, or raise an objection or restriction request. Update supported details in Profile, change notification preferences, revoke browser microphone permissions, and remove approved devices through supported security settings.

Request account and data deletion

You do not need to install the app or sign in to contact us about deletion. You may also raise privacy concerns with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children and policy changes

LonerPay is intended for adults aged 18 and over. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact support. We will update this page when our practices change and provide notice of material changes through appropriate channels.
