LONERPAY · YOUR ACCOUNT CONTROLS

Delete your LonerPay account

Request permanent account deletion and removal of associated personal data. You can start here without installing the app or signing in.

How it works

  1. Send the request below, or email support@lonerpay.com from your registered email. If you cannot access that email, explain this to support.
  2. Support verifies that you own the account. Never send your password, PIN, OTP or full identity numbers.
  3. We work with you to resolve remaining wallet money, pending payments or disputes. You may submit a request even if money remains.
  4. After verification and resolution of outstanding matters, we remove account access and delete or anonymise associated data no longer needed. We explain any required retention and notify you when processing is complete.

We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days, or explain what remains and the expected next step. A submitted request is not confirmation that deletion has finished.

What is removed or retained?

Deletion covers account/profile information, login credentials and device approvals, support data and Business Tools records/photos that are no longer required. Public store listings should be removed as part of processing. Keep any business documents you need before deletion.

Payment, accounting, verification, fraud-prevention and dispute records may need to remain for applicable obligations or unresolved claims. Support will explain the reason and applicable retention period. Provider-held records and backups can follow separate retention cycles. See our Privacy Policy.
